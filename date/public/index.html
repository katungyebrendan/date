1. Who we are

Velo ("Velo", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile dating application developed and operated by an independent developer based in Uganda. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Velo mobile application (the "App"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, including Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, the developer of Velo is the data controller responsible for your personal data. You can reach us using the details in the Contact us section below.

By creating an account or otherwise using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

2.2 Information generated through your use of the App

  • Swipes and likes — who you like, pass on, or super-like, and who likes you.
  • Matches and messages — when you match with someone, the text messages you exchange are stored so your conversation history is available in the App.
  • Profile engagement — aggregate counters such as how many times your profile has been viewed or liked, and your last-active timestamp.
  • Account metadata — an anonymous authentication identifier issued by Firebase, account creation date, and onboarding status.

2.3 Information we do not collect

Velo does not require an email address or password to sign up, and we do not collect government ID, payment card details, or precise, continuously-tracked GPS coordinates. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, display, and operate your Velo profile;
  • To show you other users' profiles and let them see yours, based on your gender, preferences, and location;
  • To power swiping, matching, and in-app messaging between matched users;
  • To enforce a single account per phone number and reduce fake or duplicate accounts;
  • To enforce our minimum age requirement of 18 years;
  • To send you local, on-device notifications (for example, when someone likes your profile);
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the App;
  • To respond to your requests, including account deletion.

We do not use your personal data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on:

  • Consent — you actively agree to our data usage practices before completing onboarding, and you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Performance of a contract — processing your profile, matches, and messages is necessary to provide the core Velo service you sign up for.
  • Legitimate interests — such as preventing duplicate accounts and keeping the platform safe and functional.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users — certain profile fields are visible to other Velo users as described in section 6.
  • With service providers — we use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage) to host and store app data. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own security and data-processing terms. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • For legal reasons — if required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • With your consent — in any other case where you have explicitly agreed to the sharing.

6. What other users can see

When your profile is complete, the following information is visible to other Velo users you are shown to (or who are shown to you):

  • Your name and age
  • Your profile photo
  • Your bio and interests
  • Your city / general location
  • Your WhatsApp phone number, once shown on your profile card so matches can call or message you directly on WhatsApp
Please be mindful: your WhatsApp number is visible to other users of the App as part of normal use of the service. Only share information you are comfortable making visible to people you match with, and use your own judgment when meeting people you've connected with through the App.

7. Location data

If you choose to use the "use my current location" option during onboarding, the App requests a single, one-time reading of your device's GPS position while the App is open in the foreground. We do not track your location continuously or in the background. That coordinate is converted into a general place name (such as your city or area) using a geocoding service, and only that place name — not your raw coordinates — is stored on our servers and shown on your profile. You may instead type your city manually without granting location access.

8. Data retention & deletion

We retain your profile, messages, and related account data for as long as your account is active, so that the App can function as intended. You can delete your account at any time from Settings within the App. When you do, we delete your profile record and photos, and release your phone number so it can be used to register a new account. Some limited records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud-prevention, or legal compliance purposes, and matched users' copies of past conversations may persist on their own device history within the App.

You can also request deletion of your data by contacting us directly — see Contact us.

9. Data security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, secured with access-control rules that restrict who can read or write which records, and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In-app messages are not end-to-end encrypted; they are stored securely on our backend so your conversation history can be displayed in the App.

10. Age requirement & children's privacy

Velo is intended solely for users who are 18 years of age or older. We ask for your date of birth during onboarding and block registration for anyone who does not meet this minimum age. Velo is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a user under 18, we will take steps to delete that account and data. If you believe a minor is using Velo, please contact us.

11. International data transfers

Our infrastructure provider, Google Firebase, may store and process data on servers located outside Uganda. Where your data is transferred internationally, we rely on our provider's contractual and technical safeguards to protect it consistently with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you;
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (most profile fields can be edited directly in the App);
  • The right to request deletion of your data;
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time;
  • The right to object to, or request that we restrict, certain processing;
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app deletion option in Settings, or contact us using the details below.
